Frequently asked questions

Who is Spin & Win designed for?

It is designed for small and local businesses such as furniture shops, salons, cafés and electronics retailers that want to manage promotional offers and customer engagement.

Does Spin & Win sell products to customers?

No. The participating business manages its own products, prices, offers and customer fulfilment. Spin & Win provides the software platform.

Who receives customer payments?

In the current configured flow, the customer pays ₹200 directly to the participating shop. The shop manually confirms it before the offer is unlocked. Spin & Win's Razorpay checkout is intended for business software subscriptions only.

Is the subscription automatically renewed?

No automatic renewal is represented here. A business can purchase the applicable plan again before or after its current access expires.

Terms of Service

These terms apply to business accounts using the Spin & Win software platform.

Platform use

Spin & Win provides software tools for businesses to manage products, promotional discounts, customer details and branded offer experiences. The participating business is responsible for the accuracy of its product information, prices, promotional terms and fulfilment of offers.

Business responsibilities

Businesses must use the platform lawfully, provide accurate information, obtain appropriate permissions for customer data, and honour the offers they publish. Businesses remain responsible for their customer relationships and sales.

Subscriptions and access

Business software plans are ₹499 per month or ₹4,999 per year. Access is provided for the purchased subscription period after successful payment. The business is responsible for renewing access when required. The platform may restrict access after subscription expiry.

Customer payments

Spin & Win software subscription payments are separate from any product payment or shop-specific advance. Such customer payments, where required by a participating business, are paid directly to and handled by that business.

Changes and support

Features may be updated to improve the service. Businesses should use the account and support channel made available to them to report issues.

Privacy Policy

Spin & Win processes information needed to provide business account and promotional offer management features.

Information handled

Business account information may include business name, account email, products, offer settings and subscription status. Customer details may be entered by a participating business as part of its offer workflow.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the platform, provide account access, manage subscriptions, support business workflows, maintain security and troubleshoot service issues.

Payments

Business subscription payments are processed through the payment provider. Spin & Win does not ask customers to send their shop purchase payments to the software provider. Payments for a participating shop's products or shop-specific advances are handled by that shop.

Sharing and security

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, database and payment functions. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data requests

Business account holders can use their account or the support channel made available to them to request help with their information. Businesses are responsible for having an appropriate basis to collect and use customer information.

Refund Policy

This policy applies to Spin & Win business software subscription payments. It does not govern purchases made directly from participating shops.

Subscription purchases

Please review the selected plan and subscription duration before completing payment. Once subscription access has been activated, subscription fees are generally not refundable except where required by applicable law or where a duplicate or erroneous charge is confirmed.

Failed or duplicate payments

Shop transactions
